# Indian regulatory and digital-infrastructure "why now" for ClaimDesk (agentic AI for hospital health-insurance claims)

Status date: 3 Oct 2026. India only. Source-quality flags: **[Primary]** = regulator/government/standards body; **[Reputable]** = mainstream business press or law-firm analysis; **[Secondary]** = vendor/insurtech blogs or aggregators, so verify before quoting externally.

## 1. IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance Business (29 May 2024) and related master circulars: cashless TATs, claim rules, waiting periods, grievance and penalties

### Takeaway
The 29 May 2024 Master Circular (IRDAI/HLT/CIR/MISC/86/05/2024) sets hard clocks on insurers: 1 hour to decide a cashless pre-authorisation and 3 hours to give final discharge authorisation. If discharge approval runs past 3 hours, the insurer pays any extra hospital charges out of its shareholders' fund. Insurers must also collect documents directly from hospitals, and no claim can be rejected without a Claims Review Committee. Separately, the Insurance Products Regulations (1 Apr 2024) cut the PED waiting period to 36 months and the moratorium to 60 months. The Protection of Policyholders' Interests (PPI) master circulars (2024) require grievances to be resolved within 14 days, and insurers pay Rs 5,000 a day if they do not comply with an Ombudsman award within 30 days. Together these rules create a measurable, time-boxed SLA that a hospital-side agent can enforce and evidence.

### Inferences
- The 1h/3h clocks and the "insurer pays from shareholders' fund" rule turn a hospital-side timestamped audit trail into money: a log of when the pre-auth was sent, when queries came back and when the discharge request was sent. ClaimDesk could auto-log TAT breaches and attach them to grievances and Ombudsman complaints.
- The rule that documents come from the hospital, not the patient, puts the documentation burden on the hospital insurance desk. An agent that assembles complete, payer-specific document packs addresses exactly this workload.
- The CRC requirement and the bank-rate+2% interest rule give a patient-side agent concrete grounds to challenge a repudiation: ask for evidence of CRC approval, and calculate interest owed.
- Because 100% cashless is an "endeavour", not a deadline, reimbursement claims will continue. That keeps a patient-side reimbursement-assist market alive.
- Between the FY24 Rs 15,100 crore disallowed and roughly 8% repudiated in FY25, there is a quantifiable leakage pool that ClaimDesk can target: deductions, non-payables and documentation-driven rejections.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the full primary PDF of the 29 May 2024 Master Circular from irdai.gov.in, so paragraph numbers are not cited. The **Customer Information Sheet** (CIS) clauses, the exact emergency-cashless wording and the number of circulars repealed were not verified.
- The health-specific claim settlement TAT in the PPI Regulations 2024 is unverified. One source says reimbursement claims must be settled within 15 days; the commonly cited general rule is 30 days from the last document, with longer periods where investigation is needed. Check the PPI master circular text directly.
- I found no 2025–2026 amendment changing the 1h/3h TATs or the 36/60-month periods.
- The FY24 cashless share reported in one snippet (66.16%) and the FY25 figure of about 58% come from different, unverified sources. Treat any year-on-year comparison as unreliable.

## 2. Proportionate deduction, room-rent capping, standard non-payables and standardised wordings

### Takeaway
Since the 2020 standardisation (effective 1 Oct 2020), IRDAI has reduced the list of non-payable "non-medical" items from 95 to 68. Many consumables are now folded into room, procedure or treatment charges. The 2024 Master Circular confines proportionate deduction to room-linked associated expenses. ICU, pharmacy, implants and diagnostics are not proportionately deducted. These rules are deterministic and well suited to automated pre-bill and post-settlement checking.

### Cited Findings
- The October 2020 guidelines cut the non-payable list from **95 to 68 items**. Some items are now subsumed into room charges (hand wash, caps, slippers, housekeeping) and procedure charges (surgical blades, gauze, arthroscopy instruments) — [Zurich Kotak (insurer)](https://www.zurichkotak.com/health-insurance-guidelines); [Basunivesh (Secondary)](https://www.basunivesh.com/irdai-health-insurance-guidelines-2020-12-rules-you-must-know/); [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/press-news/14189-irdai-reviews-list-of-non-payable-items-under-health-insurance)
- Proportionate deduction is limited to room-rent-associated medical expenses. "Non-associated" expenses such as ICU, pharmacy, consumables, implants and diagnostics are excluded from it under the 29 May 2024 Master Circular — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/spotlight/financehub/the-three-lines-in-your-health-policy-that-decide-how-much-you-actually-get-paid); [RTI Wiki (Secondary)](https://righttoinformation.wiki/proportionate-deduction-health-insurance-india)
- Non-payable categories include consumables, personal items, administrative costs, food for attendants and miscellaneous service charges — [ManipalCigna](https://www.manipalcigna.com/blog/non-payable-items-health-insurance); [Ditto](https://joinditto.in/articles/health-insurance/non-payable-items-in-health-insurance-the-2025-guide-to-save-money)

### Inferences
- The non-payable lists (Lists I–IV) and the AME/non-AME split can be encoded as a rules engine. That lets the agent flag likely deductions **before** the bill goes to the insurer (hospital side) and challenge improper deductions **after** settlement (patient side).
- Partial deductions are a large share of disputes; for example, Star Health had more than 10,000 FY24 complaints about partial or full rejection — [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/36635-41-of-health-insurance-complaints-were-resolved-in-favour-of-policyholders-in-fy25). This points to demand for deduction-audit features.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the primary circular numbers or the exact item text for the 2020 "Guidelines on Standardisation of Exclusions / List of Non-Payable Items" (Lists I–IV) or the 2020 Product Filing Guidelines. Get the PDFs from irdai.gov.in before encoding rules.
- I found no 2025–2026 revision of the non-payables list.

## 3. National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX): launch, standard, mandates, adoption, developer access, problems

### Takeaway
The National Health Authority (NHA) runs NHCX. It went live in June 2024 and uses NRCES's ABDM FHIR R4 Implementation Guide (v6.5.0, May 2025), which has full profiles for coverage eligibility, pre-auth/claim, communication and payment. As of April 2026, 83 payers and about 42.7k provider facilities were registered. Onboarding is **advisory for insurers and TPAs and not mandatory for hospitals**. NHA drives adoption through DHIS incentives that, since 2026, also pay software vendors per claim. Volume appears concentrated in PM-JAY, so ClaimDesk should treat NHCX as an emerging rail to build for, not yet the dominant one for private claims.

### Cited Findings
**Launch and operator**
- NHA built NHCX. It went online in June 2024, and HDFC ERGO processed its first claim on it on 9 June 2024 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Health_Claims_Exchange). Star Health is said to have done its first NHCX transaction in July 2024 — [Caladrius (Secondary)](https://caladriushealth.ai/blog/2026/05/15/The-ABDM-Stack/)

**Technical standard**
- The NRCES "FHIR Implementation Guide for ABDM" is at **v6.5.0** (generated 2025-05-08) and is FHIR R4. Its NHCX bundle profiles are ClaimBundle, ClaimResponseBundle, CoverageEligibilityRequestBundle, CoverageEligibilityResponseBundle, InsurancePlanBundle and TaskBundle. Its resource profiles are Claim, ClaimResponse, Communication, CommunicationRequest, Coverage, CoverageEligibilityRequest, CoverageEligibilityResponse, InsurancePlan, PaymentNotice, PaymentReconciliation and Task — [NRCES HCX profile page (Primary)](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/hcx-profile.html)
- The IG lists these flows: "Get provider/payor details, Eligibility check, Pre-auth request flow, Claims request flow, Payment notification, Payment acknowledgment, Search/fetch claims data for status checks, regulatory compliance" — [NRCES (Primary)](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/hcx-profile.html)
- Supporting PDF: "Implementation Guide for Adoption of FHIR in ABDM and NHCX" — [NRCES (Primary)](https://www.nrces.in/download/files/pdf/Implementation_Guide_for_Adoption_of_FHIR_in_ABDM_and_NHCX.pdf). Coding uses SNOMED CT and ICD-10/11 — [Caladrius (Secondary)](https://caladriushealth.ai/blog/2026/05/15/The-ABDM-Stack/)

**IRDAI position (mandate status)**
- IRDAI circular **IRDAI/HLT/CIR/MISC/124/06/2023, dated 8 Jun 2023**, is addressed to insurers and TPAs: "All Insurers and TPAs are hereby advised to test and adopt the NHCX specifications". Clause 6 says they "may" onboard. It is advisory — [Nirmitee (Secondary, quoting circular)](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)
- The May 2024 Master Circular says insurers and Councils "may endeavor to" onboard hospitals — [Nirmitee](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)
- As of Sept 2026, no law or insurance regulation compels a hospital, or its software vendor, to use NHCX — [Nirmitee (Secondary)](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)

**Adoption**
- July 2024: 34 insurers and TPAs live and about 300 hospitals — [PolicyX / Nirmitee (Secondary)](https://www.policyx.com/health-insurance/articles/irdai-new-rules-2026/); [Nirmitee](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)
- April 2026 (citing the NHA NHCX dashboard): **83 payers**, **42,687 provider facilities**, **23.4 million+ claims processed** to date — [Caladrius (Secondary, citing NHA dashboard)](https://caladriushealth.ai/blog/2026/05/15/The-ABDM-Stack/)
- March 2026: NHA recognised its first cohort of "NHCX-PMJAY Early Integrators" — [Nirmitee](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)
- Some 2025–26 explainers say "major private insurers already live, PSU insurers being onboarded through 2025-26" — [search summary of explainers (Secondary)](https://caladriushealth.ai/what-is-nhcx/)

**Incentives (Digital Health Incentive Scheme, DHIS)**
- Under Corrigendum 6 (Nov 2025–Mar 2026), facilities got Rs 500 per claim. Under Corrigendum 7 (Apr–Sep 2026), they get **Rs 200 per claim, or 10% of claim value if lower**, for AB PM-JAY claims linked to ABHA and filed in FHIR through NHCX, capped at Rs 1 crore per facility per period. The software vendor whose product raised the claim gets **Rs 10 per claim** (capped at Rs 1 crore), provided it has a valid WASA report. From July 2026, software must comply with ABDM Milestones M1–M3 v3 APIs to qualify — [Nirmitee (Secondary)](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)

**Sandbox, developer access and problems**
- NHA runs a sandbox for NHCX integration testing before go-live — [Caladrius](https://caladriushealth.ai/blog/2026/05/15/The-ABDM-Stack/). Going to production needs WASA (web application security audit) certification and ABDM milestone documentation — [Nirmitee](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)
- Reported friction: there is no formal NHCX certification programme (readiness is self-attested), adoption is concentrated in PM-JAY-empanelled hospitals, and OPD-only clinics have little urgency — [Nirmitee](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)

### Inferences
- The 23.4M claims figure is cumulative and probably dominated by PM-JAY government-scheme traffic, given the incentive design. Private insurer cashless pre-auths for retail and group policies most likely still run largely through insurer and TPA portals, email and fax. ClaimDesk should therefore support both: FHIR/NHCX where the payer is live, and portal/email automation elsewhere.
- The Rs 10/claim vendor incentive (2026) and the v3 M1–M3 gate make it worthwhile for a startup to become a WASA-audited, ABDM-integrated software vendor. That is both a revenue line and a credibility signal.
- Building internal data models on NRCES FHIR profiles (Claim/ClaimResponse/CoverageEligibility/Communication) future-proofs the product and makes "NHCX-ready" a sales claim.

### Gaps
- I could not access the live NHA NHCX dashboard directly. The April 2026 numbers come through a secondary blog. I found no split of private-insurer versus PM-JAY volume, and no figure for the share of private cashless pre-auths that flow over NHCX.
- I did not confirm the HCX protocol API specs (JWE encryption, the Swasth/HCX GitHub repos) in this session.
- I did not find the sandbox registration URL or eligibility terms for startups.
- I found no reputable press account of NHCX outages or rejection-rate problems.

## 4. ABDM (ABHA, HFR, HPR), Bima Sugam status, spec availability

### Takeaway
The ABDM rails are now at scale: more than 90 crore ABHA accounts and more than 100 crore linked health records (May 2026), plus about 3.6–4.4 lakh registered facilities. Patient identity and records can be fetched with consent. Bima Sugam, IRDAI's insurance marketplace, missed several launch dates. As of the end of September 2026 it was in phased go-live (motor, health, term), and how live it is in practice is not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Feb 2025 (PIB): 73.98 crore ABHA accounts; 49.06 crore health records linked; **3,63,520** facilities on HFR; **5,64,851** professionals on HPR; **1,59,020** facilities using ABDM-enabled software — [PIB (Primary)](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2101737&reg=48&lang=2)
- Dec 2025: 83.94 crore ABHA numbers; about 4,42,000 facilities registered under ABDM — [Nirmitee (Secondary)](https://nirmitee.io/blog/is-nhcx-mandatory-hospitals-hmis-2026/)
- May 2026 (PIB): **more than 100 crore health records linked to more than 90 crore ABHA accounts**. Linked records doubled from 50 crore in Feb 2025 within 15 months — [PIB (Primary; content via search snippet, direct fetch returned 403)](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2264241&reg=3&lang=1)
- Specs are public on nrces.in, in versioned folders (3.0.1 through 6.0.0, with 6.5.0 current) — [NRCES](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/hcx-profile.html)
- **Bima Sugam:** the project was proposed in 2022 and missed launch targets of Jan 2023, Jun 2024, Aug 2024 and Apr 2025. Its website went up in Sept 2025. It is run by Bima Sugam India Federation, which has Rs 500 crore authorised capital — [c4scourses (Secondary)](https://c4scourses.in/national-affairs/bima-sugam/)
- On 30 Jun 2026, IRDAI Chairman Ajay Seth said Bima Sugam's initial motor, health and term products would be available by the end of Sept 2026 — [Business Standard (Reputable)](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/insurance/bima-sugam-to-launch-initial-products-by-sept-end-irdai-chairman-seth-126063000744_1.html)
- Secondary sources claim the platform was "partially transactional" by mid-Sept 2026, with motor live and health following — [search summaries of joinditto / c4scourses (Secondary)](https://joinditto.in/articles/general/bima-sugam/). Another source claims health went live in **August** 2026 — [Oquilia (Secondary)](https://www.oquilia.com/news/irdai-bima-sugam-platform-2026-launch-implications). **These conflict with each other and are unverified.**

### Inferences
- ABHA-linked records (discharge summaries, lab reports) let the agent pull clinical evidence for pre-auth and claims with consent, cutting manual uploads.
- HFR and HPR IDs are the identifiers NHCX claim bundles expect. ClaimDesk onboarding should capture each hospital's HFR ID and each doctor's HPR ID.
- Bima Sugam claims features are unlikely to touch hospital cashless workflows in the near term. It is a distribution and servicing layer, so it is a "watch" item, not a dependency.

### Gaps
- No primary confirmation of Bima Sugam's actual go-live as of 3 Oct 2026, or of any claims functionality on it.
- I found no ABHA-at-admission rate for private hospitals.

## 5. 2025–2026 developments: tariffs, common empanelment, cashless disputes, TPAs, GST, Insurance Amendment Act (100% FDI), AI regulation

### Takeaway
2025–26 brought open conflict between hospitals and insurers over tariffs. IRDAI responded with a common-empanelment push (circular dated 30 Jan 2025), which the GI Council turned into a single tripartite agreement; about 2,000 hospitals had joined by FY26 and private chains are opposing it. GST on individual health premiums went to zero from 22 Sep 2025, which should expand the insured base. The Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha Act (assent 20 Dec 2025) allows 100% FDI and gives IRDAI disgorgement powers. IRDAI set up an AI Working Group in June 2026, so formal AI rules for insurers are coming but not yet in force.

### Cited Findings
**Common empanelment and tariffs**

## 6. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025: obligations for a startup acting as data processor for hospitals

### Takeaway
The DPDP Rules were notified on 13/14 Nov 2025. Most substantive obligations (notice, security, breach, retention) apply from **13 May 2027**, and consent-manager provisions from about Nov 2026. Hospitals are data fiduciaries and ClaimDesk would mostly be a data processor. Rule 6 requires the fiduciary's contract with the processor to carry the same security safeguards, including encryption, access controls and logs kept for at least 1 year. The 18-month runway (to May 2027) is a window to build DPDP-native consent and audit features as a selling point.

### Cited Findings
- MeitY notified the DPDP Rules 2025 on **13 Nov 2025** — [Hogan Lovells](https://ca.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/indias-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-brought-into-force-); [PIB document (Primary)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf). SCC Online gives **14 Nov 2025** — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/). The gap is likely gazette date versus publication date.
- Commencement: Rules 1–2 and 17–21 (the Data Protection Board and administrative rules) take effect immediately. Rule 4 (consent managers) takes effect after 12 months, around **13 Nov 2026**. Rules 3 and 5–16 and 22–23 take effect **18 months after publication, around 13 May 2027** — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/); [SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/meity-notifies-final-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/)
- Rule 3 (notice): notices must be clear and standalone, setting out the purposes, contact details and how to withdraw consent or exercise rights — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)
- Rule 6 (security): encryption, obfuscation, masking or tokenisation; access control; logs; backups. Logs and personal data must be kept for at least **1 year** to detect unauthorised access. **Contracts with data processors must include the same safeguards** — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/); [Alvarez & Marsal / search summary](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/sites/default/files/2026-01/DPDPA%20POV_1911_PB_3.pdf)
- Rule 7 (breach): affected data principals must be told without delay, with the nature, timing, impact, mitigation and a contact. The Board must also be notified. The detailed report to the Board is widely reported as due **within 72 hours** — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/); [Kratikal (Secondary)](https://kratikal.com/blog/dpdp-compliance-is-now-mandatory-for-the-healthcare-industry/)
- Rule 8 (retention): data must be erased once its purpose is served, unless law requires it to be kept, with **48 hours'** notice to the individual before erasure — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)
- Rule 10 (children): verifiable parental consent is required. **The Fourth Schedule exempts healthcare professionals and clinical establishments** under specified conditions — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/); [DPDPA Schedule 4](https://dpdpa.com/schedule/schedule4.html)
- Significant Data Fiduciaries must carry out annual DPIAs and audits, check that their algorithms are safe, and may face data localisation — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/12/26/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-key-highlights/)
- Hospitals, clinics, labs and healthtechs that handle digital patient data are data fiduciaries — [Kratikal (Secondary)](https://kratikal.com/blog/dpdp-compliance-is-now-mandatory-for-the-healthcare-industry/); see also [AM Legals](https://amlegals.com/health-data-and-the-dpdp-act-a-practical-guide/), [EY](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/pdf/2025/11/dpdp-act-and-rules.pdf)

### Inferences
- ClaimDesk would be a **processor** when it handles claims for the hospital under the hospital's purposes. It would be a **fiduciary** for any direct-to-patient features, such as a patient app or reimbursement assist. Plan for both roles.
- Expect hospital contracts (DPAs) to require Rule 6 controls, one-year log retention and breach co-operation timelines short enough for the hospital to meet the 72-hour report. Build these in from the start.
- Sending data to non-Indian LLM APIs raises cross-border transfer questions (Rule 15, subject to government-notified restrictions) and possible SDF localisation. Prefer India-region hosting or de-identification before inference.
- Purpose-limited, consent-artefact-based pulls through ABDM align with DPDP and could be marketed as "DPDP-ready".

### Gaps
- I did not verify the exact penalty amounts in the DPDP Act Schedule, which are commonly cited as up to Rs 250 crore for failing to take security safeguards, against primary text in this session.
- No guidance specific to health data was found. The DPDP Act does not create a separate "sensitive" category, and the draft DISHA health-data law remains unenacted (not verified this session).
- Whether IRDAI or NHA will issue sectoral data rules for claims processors was not found.

## 7. Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017 (as amended): limits, timelines, online filing

### Takeaway
The Ombudsman can hear claims up to **Rs 50 lakh**, a limit raised from Rs 30 lakh in Nov 2023. Complaints can be filed online (cioins.co.in), and hearings can be held by video. Awards are due within 3 months, and insurers must comply within 30 days or pay Rs 5,000 a day. Draft 2025 amendments (25 Nov 2025) would add an IRDAI appellate authority and compensation of up to Rs 20 lakh for consequential loss. They were not notified as of early 2026.

### Cited Findings
- From **10 Nov 2023**, the Ombudsman can hear complaints with claim value up to **Rs 50 lakh**, up from Rs 30 lakh — [BusinessWorld](https://businessworld.in/article/insurance-ombudsman-now-accepts-claim-complaints-upto-rs-50-lakh-500223); [ClearTax News](https://news.cleartax.in/insurance-ombudsman-offices-can-admit-policyholder-complaints-of-up-to-rs-50-lakh/9807/amp)
- Earlier amendments brought in: jurisdiction over deficiency in service by insurers, agents, brokers and intermediaries; renaming as the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen; electronic filing with online status tracking; and video-conference hearings — [GKToday](https://www.gktoday.in/government-amends-insurance-ombudsman-rules/); [Moneylife](https://mas360.moneylife.in/article/government-amends-insurance-ombudsman-rules-now-track-complaints-online/3640.html)
- Complaints can be filed online at cioins.co.in. The award is due within 3 months of receiving all documents — [Sansa Legal (Secondary)](https://www.sansalegal.com/post/how-to-file-a-complaint-against-an-insurance-company-in-india-irdai-and-insurance-ombudsman); [RTI Wiki (Secondary)](https://righttoinformation.wiki/complaints/insurance-ombudsman)
- Insurers must comply with an award within 30 days, or pay Rs 5,000 a day to the complainant (IRDAI 2024 circular) — [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/health-insurance-decide-cashless-request-in-1-hour-provide-final-authorisation-for-discharge-within-3-hours-says-irdai/74269.html)
- **Draft Insurance Ombudsman (Amendment) Rules, 2025** (released 25 Nov 2025; comments due 9 Jan 2026): an IRDAI-constituted Appellate Authority; awards of up to 100% of the award amount, capped at Rs 20 lakh for consequential loss and/or Rs 1 lakh for mental harassment; offices in every state and UT capital; mandatory online filing with Aadhaar-enabled authentication; complaints registered within 1 working day. **Not notified** at the time of writing — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/insurance-laws-and-products/1713538/draft-insurance-ombudsman-amendment-rules-2025-a-shift-from-conciliation-to-adjudication)
- Outcomes: in FY25 the Ombudsman disposed of 29,017 health complaints. 41% were resolved in the complainant's favour or recommended for settlement, down from 55% in FY24 — [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/36635-41-of-health-insurance-complaints-were-resolved-in-favour-of-policyholders-in-fy25)

### Inferences
- A patient-side ClaimDesk module could draft insurer grievances (14-day clock), escalate to the Ombudsman after rejection or no reply, and file on cioins.co.in with the evidence pack. Most private-hospital claims fall under the Rs 50 lakh ceiling.
- If the 2025 draft is notified, higher compensation and an appeal route make well-documented complaints more valuable.

### Gaps
- I did not verify primary text for the pre-conditions to filing: the complaint to the insurer first, the 30-day wait or rejection, and the 1-year limit from the insurer's reply. These are commonly cited (Rule 14 of the 2017 Rules) but were not retrieved this session.
- Whether the 2025 draft amendments were notified by Oct 2026 is unknown.
- The date of the e-filing and video-hearing amendments (the 2021 amendment versus the 2023 limit change) is not pinned to a primary source.
